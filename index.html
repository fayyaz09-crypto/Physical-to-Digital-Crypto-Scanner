Technical Indicators Guide

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures price momentum. An RSI below 30 indicates an Oversold market (Potential Buy), while above 70 indicates Overbought (Potential Sell).

EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

Tracks current market trend. When EMA 20 moves above EMA 50, it signifies a Bullish trend. Otherwise, it shows a Bearish trend.

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

Displays momentum direction and trend strength by calculating the relationship between two exponential moving averages.

Bollinger Bands

Measures market volatility. Touching the lower band signals an oversold condition, while touching the upper band signals overbought.
