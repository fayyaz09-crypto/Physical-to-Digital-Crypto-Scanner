How Indicators Work

Relative Strength Index (RSI 14)

RSI measures the speed and change of price movements. Values below 30 indicate an Oversold condition, while values above 70 indicate an Overbought condition.

Exponential Moving Average (EMA 20/50)

When fast EMA 20 moves above slow EMA 50, a BULLISH trend is confirmed. Conversely, EMA 20 below EMA 50 flags a BEARISH trend.
