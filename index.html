Technical Indicators Guide

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures momentum. RSI < 30 indicates Oversold (Buy signal), while RSI > 70 indicates Overbought (Sell signal).

EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

Tracks price trend. When EMA 20 crosses above EMA 50, it triggers a Bullish trend. Below means Bearish.

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

Shows relationship between two moving averages. Used to spot trend changes and momentum shifts.

Bollinger Bands

Measures market volatility. Touching the lower band signals oversold; upper band signals overbought.
