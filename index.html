Technical Indicators Guide

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures price momentum on a scale of 0 to 100. RSI below 30 signals Oversold (Potential Buying opportunity), while above 70 signals Overbought (Potential Selling).

EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

Tracks short and long term direction. When EMA 20 crosses above EMA 50, it confirms a strong Bullish trend. If below, it indicates a Bearish trend.

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

A trend-following momentum indicator that shows the relationship between two moving averages of a coin's price, spotting trend reversals early.

Bollinger Bands

Volatility bands placed above and below a moving average. Volatility expands and contracts, showing breakout points or overbought/oversold extremes.
